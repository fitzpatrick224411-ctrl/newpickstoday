## Core Features and IBM Watson Dark Web Monitoring

### Dark Web Monitoring with AI Analysis

Identity Guard's dark web monitoring uses IBM Watson to continuously scan underground marketplaces, forums, and trading sites for your personal information. What distinguishes this from competitors is the AI component: Watson doesn't just search for exact matches of your name or SSN. It analyzes contextual data, recognizes variations, identifies bundled credential sets, and flags emerging threat patterns that humans would spend weeks investigating.

When your information appears on the dark web, you're notified within hours rather than days. The alerts include specific details about where your data was found, what information was exposed, and concrete next steps. Watson's analysis also attempts to categorize the threat level: is this a one-time paste from a 2018 breach, or an active listing being sold by criminals right now?

### Credit Monitoring and Three-Bureau Reports

Identity Guard monitors all three major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion) for new accounts opened in your name, hard inquiries, and changes to existing credit lines. You receive daily credit reports (not just quarterly), allowing you to catch fraudulent accounts within hours of opening.

The service provides credit score tracking and alerts when your score changes significantly. While the score provided is typically a Vantage Score (not the FICO score lenders use), it serves as a useful early warning indicator.

### SSN Monitoring and Financial Account Alerts

Beyond credit monitoring, Identity Guard watches for your Social Security number being used to file fraudulent tax returns, apply for government benefits, or establish medical accounts. The service also monitors your bank and investment accounts (through optional connected accounts feature) for unauthorized transactions.

Social Security number monitoring is particularly critical; once your SSN is compromised, criminals can cause years of damage. Identity Guard's Watson-powered system attempts to detect SSN misuse within days rather than the months it might take to discover through traditional channels.

### Connected Account Monitoring

Premium tiers allow you to connect your actual bank and investment accounts to Identity Guard's platform, enabling real-time transaction monitoring. The service uses encrypted, read-only connections (never storing passwords), and can alert you to unusual spending patterns or suspicious logins.

### Restoration Services

When fraud is detected, you're assigned a dedicated restoration specialist who helps you:
- Contact financial institutions and credit bureaus
- File police reports
- Dispute fraudulent accounts
- Recover compromised funds
- Restore your credit profile

This white-glove service is included even in entry-level plans, distinguishing Identity Guard from cheaper alternatives that offer alerts only.

## IBM Watson Integration: What It Actually Does

The marketing around "IBM Watson" can feel vague, so let's clarify what this integration actually means.

**What It Does**: Watson is IBM's AI platform that uses machine learning to recognize patterns in massive datasets. For Identity Guard, Watson analyzes dark web data to:
- Identify your information even when misspelled, obfuscated, or bundled with other identities
- Detect synthetic fraud (criminals creating fake identities using combinations of your real and fabricated data)
- Classify threat severity (is this serious or a false alarm?)
- Predict emerging fraud patterns based on criminal behavior trends

**What It Doesn't Do**: Watson doesn't make decisions about whether to alert you—humans at Identity Guard review Watson's findings. It's not conscious and can't think creatively. It's excellent at pattern matching but requires human oversight for edge cases.

**Practical Impact**: Most users won't notice Watson's presence. You'll simply receive better, more accurate alerts faster than you would with traditional keyword-matching systems. The value is in reduced false positives and faster detection of sophisticated fraud.
