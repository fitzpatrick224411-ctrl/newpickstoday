## How We Evaluated These VPNs for Streaming

Not every VPN works for streaming, and plenty that used to work have been blocked. Streaming platforms like Netflix and the BBC actively detect and ban VPN IP addresses, which means a VPN that unblocked content last year might fail today. For this comparison, we focused on four core criteria:

- **Unblocking reliability**: Can it consistently access geo-restricted libraries?
- **Speed and buffering**: Does it support HD and 4K without constant buffering?
- **Server coverage**: Are there enough servers in the US, UK, and other key regions?
- **Ease of use**: Can a non-technical person set it up in under five minutes?

All three services — NordVPN, ExpressVPN, and Surfshark — are premium paid VPNs with dedicated streaming optimizations. None of them are free, and none of them come with a guarantee that they'll work forever on every platform (verify current unblocking status at each vendor's site, as this changes frequently).

## NordVPN for Streaming: Reliable Workhorse With a Large Server Network

NordVPN has built a strong reputation as one of the most consistent performers for streaming. The company operates a large server network spanning dozens of countries (verify the exact current count at nordvpn.com), with servers optimized specifically for streaming labeled clearly inside the app.

**Netflix**: NordVPN reliably unblocks US Netflix, UK Netflix, and several other regional libraries. It also supports Netflix in 4K on compatible plans, which is a meaningful differentiator.

**Disney+**: Generally reliable for US and UK Disney+ access. Some users report occasional issues depending on the specific server, but cycling through a different server usually resolves the problem quickly.

**BBC iPlayer**: NordVPN maintains UK-based servers that work consistently with BBC iPlayer, which is notoriously aggressive about VPN detection. That said, performance here can vary — no VPN has a 100% uptime guarantee on iPlayer.

**Speed**: NordVPN uses the NordLynx protocol (based on WireGuard) as its default, which delivers among the fastest speeds of any VPN currently available. Streaming in HD and 4K is generally smooth on a decent base connection.

**Worth noting**: In 2018, a server in Finland was breached due to a third-party data center vulnerability. NordVPN disclosed this publicly and has since completed multiple independent security audits. This is a real incident you should know about, even though the company's response was considered reasonably transparent.

**Netflix**: Surfshark unblocks Netflix reliably and supports a wide range of regional libraries. Performance has improved significantly over the past few years as the service has expanded its server infrastructure.

**BBC iPlayer**: Surfshark performs reasonably well on iPlayer, though anecdotally it can require slightly more server-switching than NordVPN to find a working connection. Your experience may vary.

**Speed**: Surfshark uses WireGuard by default and delivers competitive speeds, though in some testing scenarios it trails slightly behind NordVPN's NordLynx implementation (verify latest independent speed tests before purchasing).

**Worth noting**: Surfshark merged with NordVPN's parent company, Nord Security, in 2022. The two brands continue to operate independently, but users who prefer entirely separate corporate ownership should be aware of this relationship.

## Cons to Consider

No VPN is perfect for streaming, and it's worth being honest about the real downsides before you hand over your credit card.

**1. VPN blocks are an ongoing arms race.** Netflix, BBC iPlayer, and Disney+ all actively work to detect and block VPN IP addresses. Even the best VPN will have servers blocked periodically. What works today may not work next week, and there is no VPN that can honestly promise permanent, uninterrupted access to any streaming service.

**2. Speeds always take some hit.** Routing your traffic through a VPN server adds latency and reduces raw throughput. On a fast broadband connection, this is usually imperceptible for streaming. But if your base internet connection is already slow or congested, a VPN can push it below the threshold needed for HD or 4K playback.

**3. ExpressVPN is expensive relative to competitors.** ExpressVPN's monthly and annual pricing sits noticeably above both NordVPN and Surfshark. For users on a budget, or those who only need a streaming VPN occasionally, the premium price is hard to justify unless its specific features or unblocking performance meaningfully outperform the alternatives for your use case.

**4. Surfshark's iPlayer reliability lags behind.** Based on available user reports and testing, Surfshark occasionally requires more effort — trying multiple servers — to successfully connect to BBC iPlayer compared to NordVPN or ExpressVPN. This is a real and practical inconvenience if iPlayer is a priority for you.

**5. Corporate ownership concerns.** Surfshark and NordVPN sharing a parent company means users who care about corporate independence have fewer truly separate options in this tier. ExpressVPN's historical controversy around its former CTO is also a fair concern for users with high privacy requirements.
