## Security Features and VPN Protocols

Mullvad offers a focused but strong security feature set. Its apps support WireGuard and OpenVPN, the two protocols most privacy-conscious users expect. WireGuard is generally faster and leaner, while OpenVPN remains a mature option for compatibility and restrictive networks.

Core features include:

- **Kill switch:** Helps prevent traffic from leaking outside the VPN tunnel if the connection drops.
- **DNS leak protection:** Mullvad routes DNS through its own VPN infrastructure to reduce exposure to your ISP or third-party DNS providers.
- **IPv6 support and leak protection:** Mullvad has historically been better than many VPNs at handling IPv6 properly rather than simply ignoring it.
- **Multihop:** Routes traffic through two VPN servers instead of one, adding separation between entry and exit points.
- **Split tunneling:** Lets you choose which apps use the VPN and which connect normally, depending on platform support.
- **Ad, tracker, and malware blocking:** DNS-level blocking options can reduce some unwanted domains, though they should not replace a dedicated security suite or browser-based protections.
- **Bridge mode / obfuscation features:** Useful in some restrictive networks, though effectiveness can vary by country, ISP, and censorship system.

Mullvad’s apps are also open source, which is a major trust signal. Open source does not automatically mean secure, but it allows researchers and technically skilled users to inspect the code.
