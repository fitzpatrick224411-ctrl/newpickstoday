## Speed and Performance: Who Wins in 2026?

Speed is often the first thing people ask about, and for good reason. A slow VPN is a frustrating VPN, especially if you're streaming, gaming, or downloading large files.

**NordVPN** has leaned heavily into its NordLynx protocol — a proprietary implementation built around WireGuard — since rolling it out a few years ago. In 2026, this continues to be one of the fastest VPN protocols available. In most independent speed tests, NordVPN delivers consistent download speeds that hover close to your base connection speed on nearby servers, with only modest performance drops on long-distance connections. Its infrastructure runs on a RAM-only server network, which also contributes to reliability.

**ExpressVPN** introduced its own Lightway protocol, which is similarly built for low latency and fast reconnection. Lightway is open-source (verify current details at vendor site), which adds a layer of transparency that security-minded users appreciate. In practice, ExpressVPN performs excellently — particularly for streaming and bypassing geo-restrictions — though in several independent benchmark comparisons, NordLynx has edged out Lightway slightly on raw download throughput, especially on longer-distance server connections.

**The verdict on speed:** NordVPN tends to be marginally faster in raw throughput tests in 2026, but ExpressVPN remains exceptionally quick and is rarely a bottleneck for everyday use. The difference is unlikely to matter unless you're running rigorous benchmarks.

## Server Locations and Global Coverage

Having servers in the right places matters — both for performance and for bypassing geo-restrictions on streaming services.

**NordVPN** maintains a larger server fleet overall, with 6,000+ servers across 110+ countries (verify current details at vendor site). This wide distribution means you're more likely to find a low-latency server close to your actual location, and more options for accessing region-locked content globally. NordVPN also offers specialty servers: Double VPN (multi-hop routing), Onion Over VPN, and P2P-optimized servers for torrenting.

**ExpressVPN** covers 105+ countries (verify current details at vendor site) with a somewhat smaller but well-maintained server network. What ExpressVPN lacks in raw server count it partially makes up for in server reliability and smart location technology. Its MediaStreamer DNS feature also lets you unlock streaming content on devices that don't natively support VPN apps (smart TVs, gaming consoles, etc.) — something NordVPN doesn't offer in quite the same way.

Both services reliably unblock major streaming platforms including Netflix, Disney+, BBC iPlayer, and others (verify with each provider, as streaming access can change over time). ExpressVPN has historically had a slight edge in streaming reliability, but NordVPN has closed much of that gap.

## Security and Privacy: How Do They Stack Up?

This is arguably the most important category for anyone using a VPN for privacy protection rather than just streaming.

**NordVPN** is headquartered in Panama, which falls outside of 5/9/14 Eyes intelligence-sharing agreements. It has undergone multiple independent audits of its no-logs policy, most recently by firms including Deloitte (verify current details at vendor site). After a 2018 server breach in Finland — which the company disclosed publicly and addressed with infrastructure improvements — NordVPN has invested heavily in its security posture, transitioning to a fully RAM-only server network and introducing a bug bounty program. That past incident is worth knowing about, though the company's response and subsequent improvements have largely satisfied the security community.

**ExpressVPN** is based in the British Virgin Islands, also outside major intelligence alliance jurisdictions. It also maintains an independently audited no-logs policy. One notable controversy: in 2021, ExpressVPN's CIO Daniel Gericke was revealed to have been involved in a hacking operation on behalf of the UAE government before joining the company. ExpressVPN addressed this publicly and stated that the individual had no access to user data systems (verify updated details at vendor site). Kape Technologies' acquisition of ExpressVPN also raised eyebrows in the privacy community due to Kape's history with adware products — a legitimate concern worth factoring into your trust calculus.

Both providers support AES-256 encryption, DNS leak protection, a reliable kill switch, and split tunneling. Neither logs your browsing activity, connection timestamps, or IP addresses — at least according to their policies and the audits conducted to date.

## Cons to Consider

No VPN is perfect, and honest reviews acknowledge the rough edges.

**NordVPN drawbacks:**
- The 2018 server breach, while addressed and disclosed, is a real part of the company's history that privacy-focused users should be aware of.
- Its desktop apps have historically been criticized for being somewhat clunky, though recent versions have improved significantly.
- The cheapest pricing requires committing to a multi-year plan upfront, which can be a barrier if you just want to try a service month-to-month without overpaying.

**ExpressVPN drawbacks:**
- It is meaningfully more expensive than most competitors, including NordVPN, with no strong justification in terms of features for the price difference.
- The Kape Technologies acquisition and the 2021 UAE-linked controversy are legitimate trust concerns that haven't fully disappeared from community discussions.
- Fewer simultaneous connections than NordVPN, which matters for users with multiple devices or family plans.
- Its server count is lower than competitors at this price point, giving you fewer options in some regions.
