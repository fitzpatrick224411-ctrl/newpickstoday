## Speed Test Results: How Fast Is NordVPN in 2026?

Speed is often the make-or-break factor for a daily-use VPN, and this is where NordVPN genuinely earns its reputation.

Testing was conducted over multiple sessions on a 500 Mbps fiber connection, using NordLynx as the protocol. Results varied by server location, time of day, and network conditions — which is true of every VPN on the market.

**Local/nearby servers (same country or neighboring):** Speed overhead was minimal — typically a 5–15% reduction from baseline. For most tasks including HD streaming, video calls, and large file transfers, the connection felt essentially unthrottled.

**Long-distance servers (cross-continental):** Connecting to servers in Asia from a North American base, speeds dropped more significantly — typically 30–50% of baseline. This is normal behavior for any VPN over long distances due to physical routing constraints, not a NordVPN-specific failure.

**Streaming performance:** NordVPN reliably unblocked Netflix US, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, and Hulu in testing (verify current details at vendor site — streaming platform restrictions change frequently). Buffering was rare on servers optimized for streaming.

**Gaming:** Latency-sensitive gaming over a nearby server was acceptable for most genres, though competitive online gaming over VPN is never ideal regardless of provider.

Overall, NordVPN is among the fastest consumer VPNs available, particularly on NordLynx. If you previously used OpenVPN-based VPNs and found them sluggish, NordLynx is a meaningful upgrade.

## Cons to Consider

No VPN is perfect, and NordVPN has real drawbacks worth knowing before you hand over your payment info.

**1. The 2018 Security Incident**
In 2019, it was disclosed that a server NordVPN rented from a Finnish data center had been breached in 2018. An unauthorized party accessed the server, though NordVPN maintained that no user logs were compromised due to their no-logs architecture. This is an important piece of history — the company's response improved their infrastructure, but the incident itself shows that no service is immune to compromise. The fact that it took roughly a year to disclose is a legitimate criticism.

**2. Renewal Price Increases**
The introductory pricing is genuinely attractive, but renewal rates can be substantially higher. Some users report feeling surprised by the price jump when their initial term ends. This isn't unique to NordVPN, but it's a pattern worth budgeting for.

**3. Jurisdiction: Panama**
NordVPN is headquartered in Panama, which is often cited as a privacy advantage (no mandatory data retention laws, outside 5/9/14 Eyes alliances). However, some privacy advocates point out that the practical legal protections depend heavily on what data is actually retained — jurisdiction matters less if logs exist. NordVPN's audits help address this concern, but it's worth understanding rather than taking at face value.

**4. Advanced Configuration Limitations**
Power users who want granular manual configuration, Linux CLI control, or deep integration with custom routing setups may find NordVPN's options more limited than open-source alternatives like Mullvad or ProtonVPN. The apps are polished and user-friendly — but that polish comes at the cost of fine-grained control.

**5. Occasional Server Congestion**
During peak hours, some popular server locations (particularly in major US cities) can experience slower-than-expected speeds. Switching to a less-loaded server usually resolves this, but it requires manual intervention.
