# About SecurePicksToday

## Who We Are

SecurePicksToday is an independent publication focused on helping everyday people choose the right online security software. We believe everyone deserves honest, jargon-free guidance.

## Our Editorial Standards

**We are honest.** We include genuine pros AND cons for every product. If a product has weaknesses, we say so. A five-star review with no downsides is a red flag — you won't find those here.

**We are accurate.** All technical claims (encryption standards, server counts, logging policies, speed benchmarks) are sourced from independent audits, official documentation, or established testing organizations (AV-TEST, AV-Comparatives, Wirecutter, etc.).

**We disclose conflicts.** When a link on this site is an affiliate link, it is clearly marked. We earn a commission if you buy through our links — but this never changes our recommendation. If a product is bad, we say it's bad.

**We update our content.** Software changes. Prices change. We review and update articles regularly to reflect current reality.

**We do not accept payment for positive reviews.** No vendor has paid for coverage or editorial placement on this site.

## What We Cover

- VPN services
- Antivirus and endpoint security
- Password managers
- Privacy tools

## Corrections

If you spot an error — incorrect pricing, outdated information, or a factual mistake — please let us know via our GitHub repository. We will correct it promptly.
